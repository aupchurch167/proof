# A10 — Legal / compliance surface

**Not legal advice.** Engineering inventory of what the codebase collects, retains, and exposes. A lawyer still has to write the actual policies.

---

ID:          A10-01
Title:       No Terms of Service or Privacy Policy in product or repo
Severity:    High
Evidence:    Grep `privacy|terms of service|ToS|cookie` in `*.js,*.jsx,*.md,*.html`: no policy pages, no signup checkbox. `Signup.jsx` / `Login.jsx` have no legal links (read of `client/src/pages/Signup.jsx` route only). Footer of emails links `https://proofcoi.com` (`email.js:73-74`) — marketing site, not in this repo.
Impact:      Commercial launch without an enforceable ToS/DPA and a published privacy notice is a sales and regulatory blocker (state privacy laws, enterprise security questionnaires). Engineering cannot point a customer at a URL.
Fix sketch:  Host `/terms` and `/privacy` (even static). Require checkbox on signup. Files: client Signup + public routes; content is owner/counsel.
Effort:      S (eng) + owner/counsel
Test:        Signup without checkbox → 400. Pages return 200.

---

ID:          A10-02
Title:       W9 uploads are tax documents (SSN/EIN) without extra controls
Severity:    High
Evidence:    Vendor `w9Path` (`schema.prisma:155`). Upload via authenticated `POST /api/vendors/:id/documents` (`vendors.js:540-583`) and **public** `POST /api/apply/:slug` (`apply.js:85-90`). Stored on the same Spaces bucket as COIs, same 15-min signed URL, same ACL. No encryption-at-rest key separate from the bucket, no access audit on download (vendor GET just signs — `vendors.js:206-208`).
Impact:      A W9 is IRS PII (TIN/SSN). A leaked Spaces key or an org VIEWER (A2-04/A2-07) is a tax-PII incident, not “just a COI.” Apply makes this writable without a Proof login.
Fix sketch:  Separate prefix + KMS; audit every signed URL; do not accept W9s on public apply until the org is a customer you have a DPA with; retention + deletion story (A3-06). Files: `apply.js`, `storage.js`, `vendors.js`.
Effort:      M
Test:        Apply without auth cannot upload `w9`. Signed W9 URL creation writes an audit row.

---

ID:          A10-03
Title:       No data-subject export or deletion implementation
Severity:    Medium
Evidence:    User delete is “remove teammate” (`users.js:112-146`), not a GDPR/CCPA erase. Vendor delete destroys COIs (A3-02) but leaves NotificationLog/AuditLog with emails. No export endpoint (A3-06). `retainForPeriod` is stored and unused.
Impact:      “Delete my account” / “send me my data” cannot be fulfilled from the app. Enterprise questionnaires that ask for DSAR SLA will be answered “manual SQL.”
Fix sketch:  ADMIN export (A3-06) + documented operator path for full erase including Spaces keys listed from `pdfPath`/`w9Path`. File: runbook + route.
Effort:      L
Test:        Export + erase playbook executed on a fixture org leaves zero rows and zero objects for that `orgId`.

---

ID:          A10-04
Title:       No OSS LICENSE file; dependency licenses not inventoried
Severity:    Medium
Evidence:    Repo root has no LICENSE / NOTICE. Runtime deps: Express, Prisma, bcryptjs, jsonwebtoken, helmet, AWS SDK, Anthropic SDK, Resend, Google auth library, React, Vite, pdf-lib, zod, etc. (`package.json` files). Several are MIT/Apache; Anthropic/AWS are vendor ToS.
Impact:      Shipping without a LICENSE leaves default all-rights-reserved on the app (probably intended) but also no third-party notice file. Some enterprise customers will ask.
Fix sketch:  Add LICENSE for the app; `license-checker --production` output in `audit/` or `/docs/licenses.md`.
Effort:      S
Test:        `npx license-checker --production --excludePrivatePackages` committed or attached to the questionnaire.

---

ID:          A10-05
Title:       No cookie banner; auth uses localStorage not cookies
Severity:    Low
Evidence:    No cookie setter in client. Tokens in localStorage (`api.js:8-35`). Google GIS may set Google cookies on `accounts.google.com` (third-party). Helmet does not emit a cookie.
Impact:      If GIS is enabled, a cookie notice may still be required in some jurisdictions. First-party cookies are not the issue today. Do not add a banner that claims “we only use essential cookies” without counsel if GIS is on.
Fix sketch:  Counsel call. If GIS stays, mention it in the privacy policy.
Effort:      S
Test:        Privacy policy lists GIS + localStorage + Resend + Anthropic + Spaces.

---

ID:          A10-06
Title:       HIPAA: not indicated by this codebase
Severity:    Low
Evidence:    Domain is construction COI / vendor compliance. No medical claims, no NPI, no ePHI models. W9s and COIs are insurance/tax, not HIPAA covered in the typical GC use case.
Impact:      Do **not** sign a BAA or claim HIPAA compliance based on this repo. If a customer uploads a health-care COI that includes patient data (unlikely on ACORD 25), that is still not a HIPAA program.
Fix sketch:  Privacy policy: “not intended for PHI.” Refuse healthcare-covered-entity positioning until a program exists.
Effort:      S
Test:        Questionnaire answer “HIPAA: No” matches the policy.
