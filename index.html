The Problem

One expired COI can cost you everything.

If a sub or vendor on your site doesn't have valid insurance when an incident happens, you're on the hook. Most GCs are managing this with email threads, spreadsheets, and hope.

📋
Spreadsheets don't send alerts

You're manually checking expiration dates — or worse, not checking at all. By the time you notice a COI expired, there's already been work done without coverage.

📨
Chasing subs wastes your day

Sending follow-up emails, waiting on PDFs, re-sending the same request three times. That's time you don't have — and a workflow that doesn't scale.

⚠️
The liability exposure is real

An uninsured incident on your job site can mean legal exposure, project delays, and costs you can't recover. Compliance audits are brutal when your records are a mess.

How It Works

Set it up in minutes.
Let it run from there.

Proof is built for operators who don't have time to babysit software. Get running fast, then get out of the way.

01
Import your vendors

Upload your existing vendor or subcontractor list in bulk. No manual data entry — your roster is in Proof in minutes.

02
Upload their COIs

Drag and drop PDF certificates into each vendor's profile — or send the vendor a direct email request with a secure upload link. Either way, Proof tracks the expiration date automatically.

03

As expiration dates approach, Proof sends automated reminders directly to your vendors — before you even have to think about it.

04
Stay covered, always

Your dashboard shows every vendor's status in real time. You always know who's current, who's expiring, and who needs attention.

FAQ

A Certificate of Insurance is a document issued by an insurance provider that proves a subcontractor or vendor has active insurance coverage. General contractors typically require COIs from every sub or vendor working on their job sites to protect against liability if an incident occurs.
When a COI is approaching its expiration date, Proof automatically sends a professional renewal reminder email to the vendor via SendGrid. You control the reminder cadence (e.g., 60, 30, and 14 days out). Vendors receive a direct request to send their updated COI — and soon, they'll be able to reply with the PDF and have it log automatically.
